About the business

What we do

Road transport of raw materials, finished goods, chemicals and heavy machinery between industrial sites.

Who we serve

Steel, cement, auto-parts, chemical and engineering plants, plus EPC contractors that need dependable fleet capacity.

Service area

Industrial corridors across India: Delhi NCR, Pune–Mumbai, Chennai–Bengaluru and Kolkata–Jamshedpur–Odisha belt.

Our promise

Right vehicle, fixed rate per km, GPS tracking on every trip, and a 93%+ on-time record.
